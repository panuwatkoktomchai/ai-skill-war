# Product: AI companion for older people living alone

Draft for the AI Skill Wars contest (AI for Bangkok). The team edits this file as the idea firms up.

## Who it is for
- People aged 60+ in Bangkok who live alone. Many have weak eyesight, shaky hands, and little experience with apps.
- Family members who live elsewhere and want to know that the person is all right.

## What it does (candidates; pick one demo flow first)
- **Companion:** talks in natural Thai by voice, remembers what the person said earlier, and checks in each day.
- **Second memory:** "where did I put my glasses?", medicine times, appointments.
- **Scam guard:** the person reads out or pastes a message or call script, and the app says whether it looks like a scam and why.
- **Help with modern services:** step-by-step help with things like LINE, mobile banking, or booking a hospital queue.

## Fixed decisions
- The app says openly that it is an AI. The aim is that it feels natural to talk to, never that it pretends to be human.
- All text and speech the user sees or hears is Thai.
- One demo flow must work end to end on a real Android phone before any second feature starts.

## Judging (as given by the organisers)
- Functionality 30, AI 25. The rest is presentation and impact.
